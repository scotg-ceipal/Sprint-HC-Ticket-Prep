Sprint Ticket Sizer

Paste tickets. Get them ranked hardest to easiest with an hours estimate. Hours are one developer's build time, coding only. QA, review, and deployment are not included. Nothing writes back to Jira.

#TicketDescription DifficultyHours Dev days
Difficulty sets the hours: XS 4, S 10, M 22, L 45, XL 90. Calibrated against ATS actuals of 13, 16, 24, 30, and 40 hours. Type over any figure to override it.
